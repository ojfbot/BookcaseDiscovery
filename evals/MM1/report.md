# MM-1 · spine pixels and embeddings

Pre-registration: [`prereg.md`](prereg.md) (fixed at e820957, before the full run). Runner: `src/eval/mm1-cli.ts`;
helper: [`helpers/embed`](../../helpers/embed/README.md). Everything per box (strips, vectors, readings, work ids)
is private, under the data root's `private/mm/<set>/`; this page holds aggregates only.

## What shipped

- **A record for every box of RB-1's box set**: 5,757 on `pilot-000` and 1,038 on the yardstick, stored apart (OC35).
  There is one record per copy (INV-4), and every yardstick record is marked `evaluationOnly`. Records are keyed to
  the box id, the box-set hash and the model revision (INV-14), and a re-run hits the cache. Every `pilot-000`
  record with a label carries TS-0's export version and split (824 of 824 labels; none disagree on the work).
- **Two embeddings per upright strip:**
  - DINOv2 base, 768 dims, about 13 ms a strip;
  - SigLIP 2 base NaFlex, 768 dims, about 14–15 ms a strip.

  Both ran on MPS. The GPU time in all was 227 s.
- **The appearance switch** (`off | shadow | candidate | vote`) defaults to `off`, and nothing reads it. No decision
  changes; there is no keep rule.

## What the label-free checks say (pre-registered; pilot-000 is machine agreement, never accuracy)

- **SigLIP 2 separates works much better than DINOv2 on real-use crops.** Same-work pairs whose readers both agree,
  against different-work pairs (M2), give an AUC of 0.977 (0.959–0.991) for SigLIP 2 and 0.813 (0.734–0.863) for
  DINOv2. The intervals do not overlap.
  - A likely reason, which is a hypothesis, not a measurement: SigLIP 2 was trained against text, so it encodes the
    printed title on the spine. That is useful for MM-2, but it means SigLIP 2's appearance is not independent of
    what the readers read.
  - DINOv2 is the more independent "how the copy looks" signal, and it is the weaker one.
- **The copy check passes on both models (M1, pilot-000 only, 9 pairs).** The AUC is 0.923 for DINOv2 and 0.977 for
  SigLIP 2. Both are above the 0.8 surprise line. By design this is circular: F-0g picked these pairs with a pixel
  test. The yardstick has no `confirmed-by-copy` boxes.
- **The visual lookalike rate (M3), which MM-2's stress test will start from:**
  - on `pilot-000`, 6.5 % (3.3–14.2) for DINOv2 and 0.0 % (0.0–0.2) for SigLIP 2;
  - within one photo, 5.9 % for DINOv2 and 0.2 % for SigLIP 2.

  These are below the 20 % surprise line, but read them with the caveat below.
- **The yardstick is too small to read.** It has 7 text-confirmed same-work pairs and 8 same-work pairs in all. Its
  intervals span most of the range, from 0.5 % to 83.3 % for DINOv2's lookalike rate.

## Caveats (for MM-2)

- **The same-work threshold is set by near-duplicates.** 237 of `pilot-000`'s 446 same-work pairs lie across two
  photos, and overlapping shots can show one physical copy twice. The other 209 are within one photo, mostly copies
  shelved side by side. Both kinds make the same-work median high, so a low lookalike rate here does not show that
  different books never look alike. MM-2's stress test should pair different works against *different copies* of
  one work (different editions, different shelves) before any mode reads appearance.
- **The threshold sits in the middle of the same-work pairs.** The rate counts different-work pairs above that
  median. A gallery match needs a margin, not a median, and the pixels never confirm alone.
- The strip is cut from the detection rectangle, so neighbours' pixels can bleed in (F-0i's outline is recorded but
  not used to mask). That tends to make neighbouring different works look *more* alike, so it raises the lookalike
  rate rather than hiding lookalikes.
- 45 `pilot-000` strips and 10 yardstick strips are under 80 % inside the frame and are left out of the measures;
  their records are kept and marked `valid: false`.
- Every strip's long side hit the 512 px cap, so the long-side quantiles in the table below carry no information.
