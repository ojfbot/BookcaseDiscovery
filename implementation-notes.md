- **2026-11-02 (L-1).** P-2 describes the detect output as `{polygons, scores, version}`. JSON Schema cannot say two arrays have equal length, so Zod and the schema could disagree on a mismatch. - **2026-11-02 (L-1).** The card says "polygon in original-image pixels" and leaves EXIF orientation open. iPhone photos are stored landscape with an orientation tag, and detectors differ on whether they apply it. - **2026-11-02 (L-1).** The card does not fix the polygon's point count. V-1a labels and Vision's perspective crop both need four corners, but P-3's SAM masks are free-form. - **2026-11-02 (L-1).** The card does not say whether `append` checks references. Chose to refuse an event that names an unknown observation, work item or recommendation, or reuses an existing id (`LedgerError`; the refused event leaves the ledger unchanged). Likewise the unresolved reason lives on `BandAssigned` (required exactly when the band is `unknown`), not on status.
- **2026-11-02 (L-1).** The card names the event types but not their fields. Fields come from the cards that emit and read them (K-1, P-1, P-4, I-1, I-3, I-4, R-2, R-3). - **2026-11-02 (P-1).** Conductor note 1 asks to log what CoreImage and Vision do with EXIF orientation by default. The helper loads through CGImageSource and passes `.up`, so the polygon frame is the stored-pixel grid; a working copy that still carries a tag other than 1 gets a stderr warning, not a correction.
- **2026-11-02 (P-1).** The card does not say how a variant's single `text` and `confidence` come from Vision's lines, what the language is, how corners are ordered, or how a bad line is answered. Went with: top candidate per line, top to bottom, joined with a newline, confidence the mean (0 when empty); language fixed to `en-US`; corners wound clockwise starting nearest the image's top-left (a quad turned past ~45 degrees is the caller's to cover with rotations), a corner up to 2 px outside the image is clamped and further is `polygon-out-of-bounds`; a line with no readable `id` is answered under `line:<n>` because the contract requires a non-empty id. All are in `helpers/ocr-vision/README.md`; each is cheap to change.
