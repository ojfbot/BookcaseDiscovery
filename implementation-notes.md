- **2026-11-02 (L-1).** P-2 describes the detect output as `{polygons, scores, version}`. JSON Schema cannot say two arrays have equal length, so Zod and the schema could disagree on a mismatch. - **2026-11-02 (L-1).** The card says "polygon in original-image pixels" and leaves EXIF orientation open. iPhone photos are stored landscape with an orientation tag, and detectors differ on whether they apply it. - **2026-11-02 (L-1).** The card does not fix the polygon's point count. V-1a labels and Vision's perspective crop both need four corners, but P-3's SAM masks are free-form. - **2026-11-02 (L-1).** The card does not say whether `append` checks references. Chose to refuse an event that names an unknown observation, work item or recommendation, or reuses an existing id (`LedgerError`; the refused event leaves the ledger unchanged). Likewise the unresolved reason lives on `BandAssigned` (required exactly when the band is `unknown`), not on status.

- **2026-11-02 (L-2).** L-1 left open how a trigger is represented. The card says `resolved → unresolved` happens "only on a trigger event", but `catalog refresh` has no event type, and deriving a trigger from the log (a new image for which spine?) would be guesswork. - **2026-11-02 (L-2).** Named the pause cause `budget-spent`, not `budget-exhausted`: `contracts.test.ts` forbids any `exhaust` value in the schemas except `strategies-exhausted` (INV-1), and the INV-2 wording is "spent budget". The test is unchanged.
- **2026-11-02 (L-2).** The card says budget exhaustion goes to `paused` but not who decides the budget is exhausted. The ledger counts adjudications and tokens from `AdjudicationRecorded` (cache hits free) against `RunStarted.budget`. It refuses a pause with a `budget-spent` cause while those caps are not reached, and refuses a further paid adjudication once they are. It cannot count spend (`AdjudicationRecorded` carries no price), so a pause under a `maxSpendUsd` cap is accepted on the scheduler's word (I-5 decides). The last adjudication may overshoot a token cap. Conservative on both sides; I-5 may tighten it.
- **2026-11-02 (L-2).** Accounting needs runs as views, so `Ledger` gained `run(id)` / `runs()` and `ViewDump` gained `runs` (additive; L-1 tests unchanged). By the same rule as L-1's deviation 4, `append` now also refuses a second `RunStarted` for a run, and a `RunFinished` for an unknown or already-finished run. An event whose `runId` was never started is still accepted (it has no budget to spend).
- **2026-11-02 (L-2).** The status table is mine, from INV-1/INV-2 and the glossary: `needs-pixels` may go back to `unresolved` (photo arrived or request withdrawn) or on to `resolved`; `paused` goes only to `unresolved` (resume under a new budget), never straight to `resolved`; `resolved` goes nowhere but `unresolved`, and only on a trigger. A transition that takes no cause is refused if it carries one. Easy to loosen, hard to tighten once ledgers exist.
